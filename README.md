# 🛒 Olist Store Analysis

## 📌 Overview
The Olist Store Analysis project focuses on understanding customer purchasing behavior, payment patterns, delivery performance, and review trends on the Olist e-commerce platform. Using SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, multiple datasets were integrated and analyzed to generate actionable business insights through KPI-driven dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze weekday vs weekend sales and payment behavior  
- Understand relationships between payment methods and customer reviews  
- Measure delivery performance and its impact on customer satisfaction  
- Evaluate pricing, freight, and payment trends across cities and product categories  

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **SQL** – Data extraction, joins, and KPI calculations  
- **MS Excel** – Data cleaning, transformation, and aggregation  
- **Power BI** – KPI dashboards and analytical reporting  
- **Tableau** – Interactive visualizations and trend analysis  

---

## 🔄 Data Pipeline (ETL)
- **Extract**: Loaded nine CSV datasets related to orders, customers, products, payments, and reviews  
- **Transform**: Cleaned data, handled missing values, standardized fields, and derived KPIs  
- **Load**: Integrated datasets and visualized insights using Power BI and Tableau  

---

## 📂 Data Sources
Brazilian E-Commerce dataset provided by ExcelR Solutions, containing approximately **100,000 customer orders**.

**Datasets Used:**
- `olist_geolocation_dataset`
- `olist_customers_dataset`
- `olist_sellers_dataset`
- `olist_products_dataset`
- `olist_order_items_dataset`
- `olist_orders_dataset`
- `olist_order_payments_dataset`
- `olist_order_reviews_dataset`
- `product_category_name_translation`

---

## 📊 Key KPIs & Analysis

### 1️⃣ Weekday vs Weekend Payment Statistics
- Compared payment volume and value across weekdays and weekends  
- Identified customer purchasing patterns by day type  

### 2️⃣ Payment Type with 5-Star Review Score
- Analyzed payment methods associated with the highest review ratings  
- Identified payment types linked to improved customer satisfaction  

### 3️⃣ Average Delivery Time – Pet Shop Category
- Calculated average delivery days for pet shop products  
- Assessed category-specific logistics performance  

### 4️⃣ Average Price & Payment Value – São Paulo
- Analyzed average product price and payment value for São Paulo city  
- Highlighted high-value customer and seller concentration  

### 5️⃣ Shipping Time vs Review Score
- Evaluated the relationship between delivery duration and customer reviews  
- Identified the impact of shipping delays on customer satisfaction  

---

## 📈 Dashboards & Visualizations
- KPI-driven dashboards in **Power BI**  
- Interactive visual analytics in **Tableau**  
- Filters and drill-downs for business-friendly insights  

---

## 📌 Conclusion
- Credit card payments contribute significantly to order volume, indicating strong potential for **credit card–based promotional campaigns** to drive future growth  
- Despite longer delivery times in some cases, **high product quality appears to offset customer dissatisfaction**, as reflected in review scores  
- **Freight costs increase with product size**, highlighting the need for optimized logistics and pricing strategies  
- **São Paulo emerges as a prime market**, contributing the highest revenue and seller count  

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Focus on **faster and more reliable delivery timelines**  
- Promote **easy and flexible payment options**, especially credit cards  
- Introduce **product-specific offers** to improve conversions and customer retention
